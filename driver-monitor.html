How detection works

Drowsy: eyes closed longer than the set time.
Smoking: index/middle fingertips held at the mouth. This is a gesture heuristic, so eating or drinking can trigger it too. A camera cannot see a cigarette reliably.
Impaired / “drunk”: camera cannot measure alcohol. It flags unusual head swaying, tilt and heavy-lidded eyes as possible impairment. For real alcohol detection, fit the MQ-3 sensor to the ESP32 (see sketch).
